KG Board — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 September 2026

KG Board does not collect, store, or transmit anything you type. The keyboard has no internet access at all. Everything stays on your device.

What the app does

KG Board is a Kyrgyz keyboard for Android and iOS. It adds a system keyboard with the Kyrgyz letters ү, ө and ң, and lets you choose or design how that keyboard looks.

Data we collect

None. We do not collect any personal data, usage data, analytics, crash reports, advertising identifiers, or device identifiers. There are no third-party SDKs, no advertising, and no tracking of any kind in this app.

What you type

The keyboard reads your keystrokes only to insert the corresponding characters into the app you are typing in. Keystrokes are never recorded, stored, logged, or sent anywhere. The app contains no networking code and, on Android, the released app declares no permissions whatsoever — including no internet permission.

KG Board has no learning dictionary, no cloud sync, no autocorrect service, and no clipboard access.

What is stored on your device

The only thing the app saves is your appearance settings — the theme you selected, its colours, corner radius, text size, and whether key sound and vibration are on, plus your chosen interface language. This is stored locally:

  • On Android, in the app's private storage.
  • On iOS, in a shared container (App Group) so the keyboard extension can read the theme you picked in the app.

If you choose a photo from your gallery as the keyboard background, a downsized copy of that one photo is stored in the same place so the keyboard can draw it. The app uses the system photo picker, so it never gets access to your photo library — only to the single image you pick — and you can remove it again in the theme editor.

This data never leaves your device and is deleted when you uninstall the app.

iOS "Full Access"

KG Board does not request Full Access. If you ever choose to enable it in iOS Settings, it is used only so the keyboard extension can read your saved theme. Nothing is transmitted, because the keyboard has no networking code.

Children

KG Board is safe for all ages. Because we collect no data at all, we collect no data from children either.

Third parties

We do not share data with third parties, because we do not have any data to share. The app integrates no advertising networks, analytics providers, or other SDKs.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new "last updated" date. Any future release that collects data would require a new policy and an update to the Google Play Data safety and App Store privacy declarations.
